About Frew Engineering

A rural workshop that speaks farmer & operator.

Frew Engineering is a rural-based engineering business on the Ryal Bush–Wallacetown Road in Central Southland. We take on general engineering, agricultural and transport work — fabrication, repairs and maintenance — for the farms, contractors and operators around us.

You deal with the people doing the work. Bring in a job, tell us what you need, and you’ll get a straight answer and honest work that holds up out in the paddock and on the road.

  • Locally owned & based in Ryal Bush, Southland
  • General, agricultural & transport engineering under one roof
  • Honest advice, tidy work and no runaround

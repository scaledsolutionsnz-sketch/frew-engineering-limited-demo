Privacy Policy

How Frew Engineering Limited handles the information you share with us.

This is placeholder content provided as part of a website demo and does not constitute legal advice. Frew Engineering Limited should review and finalise this policy before publishing.

1. Who we are

Frew Engineering Limited is a rural-based engineering business at Ryal Bush, Southland. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information you provide when you contact us or engage our services, such as:

  • Your name and contact details (phone, email, address);
  • Details of the work or enquiry you’ve asked us about;
  • Any information needed to quote, complete and invoice a job.

3. How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, carry out and invoice work, and keep in touch about your job. We do not sell your information.

4. Sharing

We may share limited information with trusted suppliers or contractors only where needed to complete your job, or where required by law. We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure.

5. Third-party services

This website may use embedded services such as Google Maps. These providers may collect information under their own privacy policies. We link to our Google and Facebook profiles, which are operated under those platforms’ terms.

6. Access & correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Contact us and we’ll help.
